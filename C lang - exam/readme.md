### ✅ Q-3) Leap Year Checker  
**Description:**  
This program checks whether a given year is a leap year or not using a separate function.

**Concepts Used:**
- Functions  
- Modulus operator (%)  
- Logical operators  

**Logic:**
A year is a leap year if:  
- It is divisible by 4 and not divisible by 100  
- OR divisible by 400
